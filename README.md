# AI and Growth Engineering | Final Exam Study Book

A self-contained study book for the 3-hour final exam, built from the course lecture decks, reading handouts, and Excel labs.

## What is inside

- A predicted final paper (Section A MCQs, Section B long answers, Section C scenarios), calibrated from the sessional 2 format (30 marks, 2 questions, 1 hour).
- Concept sections from scratch: AI and Growth Engineering (the Applied vs Generative vs Agentic AI stack), Digital Products (MVP vs Augmented, the 8-step design process), Pricing (9 strategies plus the Pricing Game), Next Best Action, the Base Exclusion Funnel, Product Metrics, Gamification (FLYG), and the Design Your Own Product lab.
- 90 practice MCQs, 37 long-answer questions, and 30 scenario and numeric questions, all with worked answers.
- Worked, code-verified solutions for the three numeric labs (the Pricing Game +20% target, the bell-curve Base Exclusion Funnel, and the design-lab financial model).
- A scope-tagged slide gallery of all 106 source slides.
- A one-page cheatsheet.

Open `index.html` in any browser, or view the deployed GitHub Pages site.

## Build

The book is assembled by `build_book.py` from `front.html`, the fragments in `sections/`, the auto-generated slide gallery (from `slides/`), and `back.html`. Source slides and notes were extracted from the course PDFs and spreadsheets.
