One-page cheatsheet

Tape this inside your head. Every line below is a trigger for a fuller answer in the sections above. If you can expand each bullet out loud, you are ready.

AI Paradigms stack (highest priority)

  • Applied AI = Expert Systems (Enforcer: rules, certainty, compliance, risk = brittle) + Machine Learning (Predictor: probabilities, churn/credit/NBA, risk = degrades) + Deep Learning (Pattern Finder: fraud/images, risk = opaque).
  • Generative AI (Creator): makes new content, risk = hallucination.
  • Agentic AI (Orchestrator): plans and acts autonomously, risk = automates mistakes, needs guardrails.
  • The line: fit-for-purpose, not one-size-fits-all; GenAI expands the toolbox, it does not replace ML or Applied AI.
  • GIGO: AI amplifies dysfunction; it rewards data integrity, not intention. Autonomy without control == Risk.

CVM and growth

  • CVM = grow the lifetime value of existing customers. Keep investing while CAC < CLV; CAC > CLV is "Start-up Demise". k = tenure.
  • 5 imperatives: mobile mind shift, big data to insights, transform CX, digital disruptor, embrace AI.
  • Data Mix: WHO = demographics, WHEN = in-market timing (3rd-party cookie decline), WHAT = transactions, WHY = surveys.
  • Growth types: Promotional (short-term) vs Emotional (durable loyalty). Sustainable growth needs more emotional.
  • Segmentation: macro (demographics) / strategic (long-term) / micro (CLV, RFM, IBRO, cohort, ML-DL unsupervised). ATL = mass, BTL = targeted.

Experiments and hypothesis

  • Target vs Control = communication effect (stratified sampling, apple to apple).
  • A/B test = best message (champion vs challenger).
  • Model Performance = modelling effect (random vs high-propensity).
  • Total value = doing nothing vs running all three.
  • Null = no change; Alternative = change. Directional (one-tailed) = uplift only; Non-directional (two-tailed) = up or down. Decide only with statistical significance.

Digital products

  • Products = tangible; Services = intangible. The market rewards relevance.
  • MVP/Basic = minimum for the core benefit; Augmented = beyond minimum expectations.
  • 4 types: Digital Goods, Retail/Distribution, Augmented Products, Digital Services.
  • Two I's: Interactivity (it reacts, gathers data) and Individualization (personalized prices/promotions).
  • 8 steps: Idea Generation, Idea Screening, Concept Development and Testing, Marketing Strategy and Business Analysis, Prototype, Iterative Testing, Commercialization, Breakeven. First-mover and fast-follower advantage; S-curve lifecycle.

Pricing (9 strategies)

  • Cost-Plus, Competitive (red-ocean risk), Penetration (low then raise: Netflix, Uber), Dynamic (surge: airlines, hotels), Price Discrimination (location-based), Economy (Walmart, IKEA, Southwest), Premium (Apple, Rolex), Loss Leader (Kindle, Gillette), Value-Based (the holy grail).
  • Digital nuance: near-zero marginal cost, so cost-plus and dynamic are less relevant; value-based wins.
  • Pricing Game: Revenue = Customers x Subscription Amount x Pricing rate. Orange = 5% subscription cut (max 2 segments). Target = +20% revenue. The 5% cut lowers revenue, so the price rise must over-compensate.

NBA and the Base Exclusion Funnel

  • NBA = right offer, right customer, right time, right channel. Four tiers: Understand customer, Decide offer, Place/Pitch on channel, Adaptive learning and self-healing. Dormancy 0 to 150 days. Results: 2 to 18 products, activations 11% to 19%.
  • Funnel: Combined Score 0 to 400; mu = 170.85, sigma = 103.12, range 1 to 368. mu +/- 2 sigma = [-35, 377] is wider than the data, so it excludes nobody; practical limits near 20 and 350.
  • Flags: Qualify (inside limits), Slide back (top 3 model deciles), Retained (Qualify or Slide back), Final (Retained and not fraud).
  • Retained: 90.42% (score trim) to 93.31% (slide-back) to 89.11% (after fraud removal).

Metrics, gamification, and the design lab

  • Metrics: CAC; CLTV (approx ARPU x lifetime, lifetime approx 1/churn); DAU/MAU; Stickiness = DAU/MAU; MRR/ARR; ARPU vs ARPAU (per active user); Churn vs Retention; NPS; CSAT; FFI; Conversion. Product Teardown families: UX/engagement, business/growth, functionality/technical, retrospective.
  • Gamification (FLYG): PLAY, LEARN, EARN. Freemium plus partnerships plus trust-account interest. Four-tier objectives: affordability, inclusion and education, credit-risk data pipeline, bundle benefits.
  • Design lab: investment product for daily wagers, 30-day eligibility, Basic/Intermediate/Advance, primary leg = re-investing the invested amount. Nine-part teardown. Two guiding questions: what are you building, and how do you know it is good.
The two guiding questions run through everything. 1) What product are you building? 2) How do you know if your product is good (which metric proves it)? Close every product answer with these.
