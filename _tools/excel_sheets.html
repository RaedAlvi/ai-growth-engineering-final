Product Design Lab Instructions
  1. Think of the Pakistani financial services market and the dynamics of the country.
  2. For financial inclusion of daily wagers, design an investment-based product (investment to stay 30 days to be eligible).
  3. Design 3 options (Basic, Intermediate, Advance), each with different eligibility.
  4. Primary profitability leg: re-investment of the invested amount by your organization (annual profit to org; customer profit monthly).
  5. Secondary profits via innovation and partnership models.

Base Exclusion Funnel.xlsx — Sheet1 (completed)
Instructions
  1. From the Score (theoretically 0 to 400), pick the min and max. Apply Bell Curve Ref (mu +/- 2 sigma) to retain 95% of the base, to identify the threshold.
  2. Apply the threshold on the practical max and min to get the upper and lower limits of score; keep the base within them.
  3. Then slide back excluded subs in the top three model deciles. Remove fraud (gaming) subs. Fill the two summary tables.

Data Flags (binary)

Qualify = 1 if lower limit < score < upper limit. Slide back = 1 if in top 3 model deciles. Retained = 1 if (Qualify + Slide back) is non-zero. Final = Retained and not a fraudster.
